# Source quality rules

Use the strongest accessible source for each claim:

1. Primary or official source: issuing authority, original dataset, standard, filing, or vendor documentation.
2. Reputable secondary source: established publication or institution that links to the primary record.
3. Discovery-only source: search result, aggregator, forum, or repost. It may locate a source, but cannot alone verify a material claim.

Record the URL, title, retrieval time, and the exact source ID used in the report. If two sources disagree, keep both claims and describe the conflict instead of selecting one silently.
